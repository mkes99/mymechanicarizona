Batteries/check-engine work exist on the homepage, but the user required /services/ for listed services.

- A/C/Cooling: removed heating concerns, radiator-specific service and coolant-leak-specific services; use A/C Service And Repair and Coolant System.

- Removed all six non-source “Before your visit” descriptions and the claim that findings/recommended work will be discussed before repairs. Replaced with live appointment-request/confirmation instructions, with the form reference adapted to the appointment link.
- Removed the authored “Know what to ask” section: maintenance-schedule guidance, package-fit advice, cold-start/idle/acceleration diagnostic advice, fault-code explanation, brake-noise/vibration advice, pothole/tire-history advice, cabin-versus-engine cooling explanation, cooling-at-idle questions, shifting/leak guidance, repair-decision guidance, used-car purchase/access advice and road-trip scheduling advice.
